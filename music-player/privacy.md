# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

This policy explains what Music Player: MP3 & Visualizer ("the app") does with information, made by Jeffrey Tan Wai Hock, an individual developer in Malaysia ("we", "us"). In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we run no server that collects or stores anything about you.

## Your music

The app reads the songs on your phone (Android's "Music and audio" permission) to list and play them, with their covers, tags and the lyrics stored in the files. They are read on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere, except when you choose to share or play them with others as described below.


## What stays on your phone

Your playlists, listening counts (used for suggestions and Most played), settings and whether you own Music Player Plus are kept on your phone only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's settings in your phone's backup to your Google account; that backup is Google's, and we cannot see it.


## Listen together

When you host a session, friends who join can play the songs in your queue from your phone, directly: over the Wi-Fi you share, or phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct (Google Play services' Nearby Connections) when there is no shared Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through a server. Only people with the session's code, QR code or invite link can join.

When you join a session, the host sees your phone's name (as set in Android's settings) in its list of listeners.

Android requires the location and "nearby devices" permissions to find phones nearby over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. The app uses them only for that; it does not read, keep or send your location.

If you install the app from an invite link, Google Play passes the invite on to the app so the session opens on first launch. It is read on your phone and not sent anywhere.


## Sync from PC

With the PC app, your computer sends songs to your phone directly over the Wi-Fi you share.

With the web page (sync in a browser), the page and the phone meet for a few seconds on our connection server, run on Cloudflare, only to set up a direct connection between them; Cloudflare's and Google's connection services (STUN) help them find each other. These services see your internet address, as any website does. The songs then go straight from the computer to the phone and never pass through a server. The server keeps no record of the songs, your phone or the page.


## Online music and links

- Audius tab: your searches and the tracks you play are fetched from Audius (api.audius.co and its network), which sees your internet address and what is asked for, as any website does. The app sends no account or personal details. Audius's own privacy policy applies to that: https://audius.co/legal/privacy-policy
- Radio tab: the list of stations comes from Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free directory; a station you play streams from that station's own server. Both see your internet address. Your favourite stations are kept on your phone.
- Find lyrics and Find on YouTube open Google search or YouTube with the song's title and artist; those services' own policies apply there.


## Camera, sharing and notifications

- The camera is used only to scan an invite's or a PC's QR code, on your phone; no picture is kept or sent.
- When you share a song, a picture or a link, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick.
- Notifications show what is playing, with its controls.


## Purchases and ratings

Music Player Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app only learns whether Plus is owned. If you rate the app, the rating goes to Google Play, not to us.


## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.


## Changes

If the app starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated first, with a new date above. The same text is in the app (Settings → Privacy policy).
